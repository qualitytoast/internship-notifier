# Freshman & sophomore programs

Every freshman/sophomore-friendly program found at the companies InternWatch watches, compiled on
2026-10-20 (the companies added in November 2026: researched on 2026-11-09, discovery/candidates/underclassmen_research.md) from the company career sites, the postings InternWatch has stored, and the lists under
[Sources](#sources). Dates are for the summer 2027 cycle as those sources reported them in October 2026;
check the company's page before applying.

**For:** Fr = first-year, So = second-year. You are class of 2029, so this cycle the **So** and **Fr + So**
rows are yours.

**InternWatch:**
- **emailed:** the posting is in a feed InternWatch reads and passes the filters (tagged `freshman/sophomore`).
  Programs whose titles have no student word are caught by the freshman/sophomore markers in
  `config/filters.yaml` (sophomore, first-year, discovery program, insight programme, "Class of 2029", …) or by
  the company's `role_keywords` in `config/companies.yaml`.
- **not watched:** the program is only on its own web page or an outside site, not in any job feed, so no
  email will come. Apply on the page.
